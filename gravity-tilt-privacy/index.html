Privacy Policy: Gravity Tilt

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Gravity Tilt mobile game ("the app") collects and how it is used. The app is published by CCSS Studio ("we", "us").

Information we collect

Game progress. Your progress (stages, coins, items, settings) is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive this data.

Advertising. The app shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for in-game rewards, and an occasional full-screen ad between levels. The between-level ads can be turned off with the "Remove Ads" purchase. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which can be used to estimate your general location), how you interact with ads, and general device and diagnostic information, to show and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud. This data is encrypted in transit. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play (and Apple App Store on iPhone). We use RevenueCat to verify purchases and restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts, not your payment details. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location or photos.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can turn off personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all game progress stored on your device.
  • To ask us to delete the purchase records linked to your anonymous app user ID at RevenueCat, email us at the address below.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
